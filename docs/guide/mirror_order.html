Bonus: ask for a "front-surface mirror" (silver on the front, no double image). Most shops won't have it, and plain 2 mm is fine.

Check at the counter

  • Lay each piece on the template: same size, corners square.
  • Hold it near your eye and look at a straight edge: one sharp line, no double edge.
  • Hold it by the edges only.
