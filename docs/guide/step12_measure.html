Step 12: measure before 3D printing

HUD glasses · Charan · fill in by hand · all sizes in mm
Tools: ruler, calipers, phone camera, t3_oled_hello uploaded on USB

A. Optics bench (do in order) — no mirror

  1. Hold the lens in front of the OLED. Slide the OLED until HELLO is sharpest.
    Measure OLED screen → lens centre: u = mm  (bench: 22)
  2. Put the acrylic at 45° about 25 mm behind the lens. Still sharp?  ☐ yes  ☐ no
  3. Text reads the right way round with MIRROR_X =  ☐ 0  ☐ 1  (flip, upload, look again)
  4. Readable in normal room light?  ☐ yes  ☐ no  (if no: raise contrast, dark paper behind acrylic)
  5. Lens real focus: project a bright window onto paper, sharp image → lens to paper f = mm
