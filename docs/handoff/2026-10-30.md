- Possible frame clash: tube floor is ~15 mm above the acrylic centre; a tall glasses rim may hit it.

## Fixes
- Boards held by 3-sided pockets + lid fingers on the USB-C shells instead of hooks.
- Clip jaw sized by a strain check (`jaw_t` 1.6, `jaw_lip` 0.5 → 1.9 %); the render fails above 2.5 %.
